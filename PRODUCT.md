# Product

## Who it is for
- Hiring managers and engineers screening a junior backend or full-stack developer.
- Freelance clients deciding whether to hire me for a web, API or AI automation job.

## What they should leave with
1. I build systems where correctness matters, and I can explain why.
2. Every project has a live demo and its code, and both work.
3. How to reach me, and a CV they can download or print.

## Rules for content
- Plain language. No em dashes.
- Each project shows one hard problem, the fix, and the code or a drawing of it.
- Work for employers: job title and dates only. No client or product names.
- Only projects that are live and public. Check every link before publishing.
